A procedurally generated dungeon crawler.
# Controls
- W/Up Arrow: Move Forward / Flick Enemy Tooth
- S/Down Arrow: Move Backward / Block Downward
- A/Left Arrow: Turn Left / Block Left
- D/Right Arrow: Turn Right / Block Right
- Use mouse to open the map and game log, eat arms and select menu items.

# Description
Arm to the Teeth is a randomly generated dungeon crawler inspired by games like Baroque and the Wizardry series. In Arm to the Teeth, you must fight your way up the floors off the dungeon and fight against its creation using the only weapon available, your arms. To replenish your health and hunger, you must consume your arms at the cost of your teeth and your offensive capability. Keep looking for more resources and keep ascending the dungeon, if you can.
